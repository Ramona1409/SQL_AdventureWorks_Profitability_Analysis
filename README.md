# AdventureWorks Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales profitability using the AdventureWorks 2022 database. The objective is to understand how revenue, gross margin, discounts, seasonality, and product performance influence business profitability.

## Business Objectives

Analyze revenue and gross margin across product categories.

Identify seasonal variations in profitability.

Explore the relationship between discounts and gross margin.

Identify products with high and low profitability.

Provide business recommendations to improve profitability.

## Tools & Technologies

**SQL** – data querying and analysis

**Microsoft Excel** – data analysis, tables, and visualizations

**AdventureWorks 2022** – source database

## Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Seasonal Profitability

Analyze monthly variations in gross margin and discounts across product categories.

### 2. Revenue and Gross Margin Trends

Compare annual revenue and gross margin from 2011 to 2014 across Bikes, Clothing, Accessories, and Components.

### 3. Discount Impact

Explore how discounts relate to gross margin and identify opportunities to optimize pricing strategies.

### 4. Product Profitability

Identify high-performing products, including Mountain-200 models, and products with negative gross margins, including selected Road-650 and Touring models.

## Key Findings

Bikes generates the highest revenue and gross margin but shows substantial fluctuations.

Clothing has comparatively low gross margin.

Accessories demonstrates relatively stable profitability.

Components has comparatively low gross margin despite significant sales volume.

Certain products have negative gross margins, highlighting opportunities for further investigation.

## Business Recommendations

Review discount strategies for Bikes, particularly during periods of strong demand.

Investigate the cost structure and pricing of Components.

Maintain the effective practices observed in Accessories.

Prioritize products with strong gross margins.

Evaluate low-margin and loss-making products before making pricing or portfolio decisions.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of SQL and Excel to explore sales data, evaluate profitability, identify business trends, and develop data-driven recommendations.
